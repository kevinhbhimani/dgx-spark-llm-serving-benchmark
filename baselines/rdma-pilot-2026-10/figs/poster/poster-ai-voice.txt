This poster examines LLM serving performance on DGX Spark, comparing single-node inference with tensor and pipeline parallelism across two Sparks connected by 200 Gb/s RoCEv2. Using vLLM and a four-bit Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B model, it evaluates throughput, latency, and requests meeting latency targets across increasing concurrency. In this single-run study, pipeline parallelism delivers the highest throughput at peak load, while tensor parallelism serves the most requests within latency targets.
